<p>The Student Acad Cred Detail screen shows information about a particular student acad cred record. You can get to the SACD screen via the SPRO screen. Below is a screenshot as well as detailed information about this screen.</p>
<ol>
<li>Course sections are made from courses; so this gives the ID of the course, the name of the course, and the section number of the course section.</li>
<li>Short Title of the course</li>
<li>Subject of the course</li>
<li>Department of the course</li>
<li>Academic level and course level of the course</li>
<li>If the course is offered at the home institution, then it will be an institutional credit.</li>
<li>Start and end dates of the course</li>
<li>Term and reporting term of the course</li>
<li>Attempted credits and completed credits</li>
<li>Grade and the calculated grade points based on completed credits and grade</li>
<li>Registration status</li>
<li>Registration date and time</li>
</ol>
