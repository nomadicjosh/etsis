Below are the following rules that pertain to add, drop and withdrawal statuses in connection with the add/drop dates specified on your terms.

If the posted status is ‘W’ or ‘D’ and today’s date is less than the primary term start date, then delete all student course sec records as well as student acad cred records.

If posted status is ‘W’ or ‘D’ and today’s date is greater than equal to the primary term start date, and today’s date is less than the term’s drop/add end date, then delete all student course sec as well as student acad cred records.

If posted status is ‘W’ or ‘D’ and today’s date is greater than equal to the primary term start date, and today’s date is greater than the term’s drop/add end date, then update student course sec record with a ‘W’ status and update  student acad record with a ‘W’ grade and 0.0 completed credits.
