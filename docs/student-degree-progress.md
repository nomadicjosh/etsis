# Student degree progress review

Open **Students → the student → Student programs → Degree requirements → Degree progress**. This on-demand review brings the general degree minimums and proposed course-group placement into one workspace. Select a published degree for the pursuit first if none is assigned.

All comparisons use the same verified current-work set, selected publication and applicable approved exceptions within one student lock and read transaction. This is a current review, not a saved evaluation, complete degree audit, academic-standing determination or graduation decision.

## General minimums

The review compares four effective thresholds with the retained calculations:

| Minimum | Facts used |
| --- | --- |
| Total earned credits | Eligible institutional and transfer awards after the retained repeat-credit policy. Group sharing and waivers create no additional earned credit. |
| Resident credits | Institutional-origin earned-credit bounds from that same award calculation. A tied institutional/transfer award remains a range instead of choosing whichever origin would satisfy the minimum. Additional residency exclusions are outside this check. |
| Cumulative GPA | Institutional work only. Transfers are excluded **before** applying the retained repeat-GPA policy. |
| Overall GPA | The retained repeat-GPA and transfer-GPA choices, including only accepted transfer grades explicitly eligible for GPA when inclusion is selected. |

These scopes follow the [degree-audit specification](degree-audit-plan.md). They do not substitute a new policy for the student's selected publication. An unknown overall transfer-GPA choice can prevent overall GPA while a supported institutional-only GPA remains available. Unknown transfer grades can also prevent a complete earned-credit total without erasing independently known cumulative GPA.

GPA uses retained attempted credits and weighted quality points. It requires the applicable grading-publication context; matching display maxima or today's 4.0 default never establishes compatibility. A positive GPA denominator with zero quality points is a defined GPA of zero. No eligible GPA credits means GPA is not yet established. Missing/conflicting inputs remain unavailable, with no partial GPA or substituted zero.

GPA is compared at the supported three-decimal threshold precision without rounding upward. For example, 2.999999 does not meet a 3.000 minimum.

## Understanding the cards

- **Minimum met:** the supported known value meets the effective threshold. For a resident-credit range, its lower bound must meet the minimum.
- **Below minimum:** the supported known value is below the threshold. For a resident-credit range, even its upper bound is below the minimum.
- **Needs review:** required facts are unavailable or a resident-credit interval spans the threshold. A required GPA with no denominator instead displays **GPA not yet established** and explains why.
- **No minimum required:** the threshold is blank or zero. This creates no extra condition requiring a GPA denominator and never displays an undefined GPA as zero.

An approved reduction shows the effective minimum and the original **published minimum** from the selected version. A later draft or publication cannot replace those displayed facts. Minimum exceptions remain specific to the approved degree version and pursuit; publishing another degree does not silently copy an exception.

Minimum checks are independent components. A known cumulative GPA can be displayed alongside an unavailable overall GPA or earned-credit total. Supported [shared-course shortages](student-course-placement.md#shared-course-shortages) now identify competing groups directly in this same review; the detailed group view reuses those explanations without another input request. There is no aggregate “eligible to graduate” result. Meeting these minimums does not establish course-group, specialization, residency-policy or final award completion.

## Next steps for this review

Expand **Next steps for this review** for local guidance based on the same captured result. It points to earlier-history checks, changed matriculation context, unavailable minimum checks, known shortfalls, course-group placement, pending grades and unused completed work when those conditions are present. An unavailable or limited review offers a controlled-review path without interpreting partial academic facts.

Student progress guidance stays inside etsis by project direction. The guide does not load more records, call an AI service, recalculate totals, recommend specific courses or save decisions. Catalog-authoring AI permissions do not authorize sending this review to a provider. Returning from contribution or placement views reuses the same guidance and snapshot; **Refresh degree progress** obtains a new result. Even when displayed minimums and group placements are satisfied, the guide directs staff to verify remaining institutional requirements with the registrar rather than declaring graduation eligibility.

### Dockable local guide

In the staff **Degree progress review**, choose **Open degree progress guide** for contextual explanations and links to the detail already captured in that review. Topics cover next steps, credits/GPA, course placement, unused/pending work and what the review can confirm. This is local guidance, not an AI conversation: it makes no provider calls, discovers no additional records and saves no academic decisions.

**Move guide left/right** changes both the visible position and reading order. Narrow screens stack the guide before or after the review. Topic selection and movement work with the keyboard; **Escape** inside the guide closes it and returns focus to its launcher. Escape elsewhere retains the existing close-review behavior. The inline **Next steps for this review** and the guide reuse the same list, without duplicate guidance.

Detail links reuse the captured contribution and course-group results. Returning to degree progress restores the guide's topic, position and focus. **Refresh degree progress** requests a new authorized review and starts with the guide closed; closing the review removes the guide. Its state stays in the open review's memory, with no browser storage, persistent cache or new endpoints. No migration or permission grant is added. The guide does not declare graduation eligibility or treat a verification/search limit as an academic failure.

The guide is shared with the student portal. Staff access still uses the existing student/program scope; portal access strictly verifies ownership, including when the account also has staff privileges. Guidance for other workflows remains on the roadmap.

## Inspecting the same result

**Inspect credit and GPA contributions** shows each current record's earned-credit, cumulative-GPA and overall-GPA treatment. Transfer exclusion in the cumulative column describes the institutional-only scope; it does not claim that the institution's overall transfer policy excludes transfers. Contribution cards page 20 records at a time.

## Unused and pending work

**Review unused and pending work** opens the same contribution workspace with local filters:

- **All work:** every current record in the captured review.
- **Unused completed:** completed passing records not used in the proposed complete course-group plan. A different valid plan may use different courses. This is not a decision that these courses are unnecessary, excluded from a degree or excluded from earned credits/GPA.
- **Awaiting grades:** known ungraded institutional attempts with no completed award or quality points. The summary totals their recorded attempted credits across all posting terms, including repeated attempts; it does not predict future earned credits, repeat-policy winners, GPA or current-term enrollment load.
- **Placed or alternatives:** records supporting proposed group placements, with tied evidence alternatives identified. An alternative is not a uniquely chosen award, and group placement does not create another overall credit/GPA contribution.
- **Other outcomes:** non-passing work, late drops, voided transfer awards, unavailable grade evidence and records without a completed earned award. These are not silently relabeled pending or unused completed courses.

Unused/placed filters are unavailable unless the combined group search found a complete supported plan. Infeasible, interrupted, unsupported and needs-review outcomes return no speculative placements or unused-course count. Independently known pending-grade facts remain inspectable. Zero-credit passing completions can still be completed work; count labels refer to academic records rather than deduplicated course identities or credit awards.

When the review spans several posting terms, **Posting term** narrows the local work list. It does not change the degree minimums, calculated GPA, original all-term pending summary or group search. Each card identifies whether its posting-term label was retained; missing labels are not replaced from today's calendar. Filters, term changes and paging reuse the same captured read and reset paging when their scope changes. No additional request or event replay is performed.

Known ungraded attempts remain distinct from recorded grades whose retained definitions are unavailable, and from unexplained quality points. Such unknown evidence does not enter the pending total. A numeric limit leaves that total unavailable rather than returning a partial amount or zero. Failed input verification still returns no partial work review.

The workspace retains keyboard access, pressed-state filter labels, focus after filter/term changes, narrow-screen reflow and escaped course/group labels. No work is stored in browser storage or used to authorize a later academic mutation.

## Recorded grades and supporting coursework

The contribution review now includes each accepted record's grade code, per-course grade points and recorded earned-credit amount, separately from overall credit/GPA treatment. Unknown definitions and pending institutional grades stay distinct; known zero-point grades do not become unknown. Unretained posting-term labels are withheld instead of borrowed from today's calendar. No grading maximum or completion date is guessed.

Within **Inspect course-group placement**, each proposed placement has a [Supporting coursework](student-course-placement.md#supporting-coursework) drilldown with its recorded grades, terms, source and completion-date evidence. The owner portal offers the same view under **Course-group details**, and its work table includes recorded grades and award credits. Tied alternatives remain separate choices for inspection; multi-award pools retain their own twenty-record pages. These inspections perform no new read/replay, policy calculation or write. Temporary draft/published audit tests reuse the same evidence.

Portal evidence links use only positions in that response's curated work array. They are neither database identities nor inputs accepted by any command, and they are never persisted. Mapping rejects missing/duplicate records, invalid evidence options and more than 20,000 supporting references over at most 200 work records. Grade/scale IDs, raw work/stream IDs, reasons, actors and raw rule expressions remain private. Ownership is checked before and after the canonical read; staff use their existing authorized workspace. Earlier captured reviews remain unchanged when a fresh review includes a later grade correction. Issued documents and authoritative history are unaffected.

Recorded grades marked **Unchanged grading re-publication verified** retain their original publication. The review has verified the entire earlier/later definition within that same scale identity; it has not converted the grade or substituted today's grading policy. Changed score bands, points, credit/GPA flags, different scale identities and unknown references remain review issues. See [grading re-publication safeguards](grading-scale-setup.md#unchanged-re-publications-in-degree-reviews).

## Applied requirement checklist

When course-group placement is complete, **Inspect course-group placement → Inspect group …** shows [actual group quantities and per-card coverage](student-course-placement.md#applied-requirement-checklist). Card totals use only their eligible awards, not the whole group's credit total. Overlapping cards and shared groups do not create extra earned credits or GPA. Waived course obligations remain separate from actual completed work. Unknown or incomplete plans display no applied checklist or substituted zeros.

The student portal offers the same group quantities and a **Requirement checklist** on demand. Opening it or paging its twenty-item list uses the captured review, with no extra request. Student responses contain numeric coverage and requirement kinds; internal rule/work/course identities, saved predicates, approval reasons and actors remain withheld. The checklist is an explanation of a proposed plan, not a retained audit or graduation decision.

## Inspecting group placement and refreshing

**Inspect course-group placement** opens the [existing placement review](student-course-placement.md) from this same captured result. Staff can inspect group quantities, retained named-course lists, waivers, approved substitutions, grade floors, repeat/sharing treatment and equivalent evidence alternatives. Returning to degree progress does not rerun the read.

Each group's [Named-course review](student-course-placement.md#named-course-review) now connects its retained named requirements to matching attempts, including pending and insufficient-grade work, while keeping course options and waivers separate. The student portal exposes the same inspection with private response-local evidence links. Matching eligibility remains distinct from a complete proposed placement.

The placement review also [explains clear shortfalls](student-course-placement.md#understanding-shortfalls) in eligible credits, distinct-course quantities and named/choose evidence. These optimistic evidence bounds do not assign partial placements or declare groups complete. Missing/conflicting inputs remain needs-review outcomes.

**Refresh degree progress** requests a fresh review after registration, grade or approval changes. A grade correction changes refreshed current calculations; it does not mutate a previously returned snapshot. The snapshots are not retained audit evidence and must not be reused for a later command as if the student were still locked.

Earlier unknown history and a changed matriculation cohort have explicit notices. The review checks available surviving sources, not absolute completeness of the historical archive. Missing historical equivalence, unsupported group rules and incomplete search results remain visible. A group search limit can leave independent minimum comparisons available, while returning no speculative group placement.

## Consistency, bounds and privacy

The workflow verifies owner or `sis:student:read` access and the student-program scope before collection and before returning. Selected requirements, approved exceptions, academic inputs and required chronology share the existing 400-page/2,000-event replay allowance. Academic input discovery remains capped at 200 distinct sources; placement preparation/search retain their existing fixed bounds. The two GPA scopes use the same already-resolved attempts, so they add no second academic discovery or chronology replay.

A read failure or preparation/replay limit returns no partial minimum cards, contribution records or group plan. A corrupt/mismatched source produces a safe registrar-review error after the read transaction aborts. Requests cannot supply alternate minimums, GPA scopes, selected policies or larger work budgets.

Browser requests retain CSRF protection, private/no-store responses and debug suppression. Early-drop references are checked internally and omitted from current counts, contribution records and returned placement evidence. Raw events, actors, reasons and internal source references are not returned. Drilldowns use memory in the open review, with no browser storage or persistent result cache.

Private POST endpoint: `/sis/students/{student}/programs/{assignment}/degree-requirements/progress/`.

No migration, stored policy/event conversion, new event table, academic write or retained evaluation is introduced. Full historical equivalence/chronology coverage, specialization/exclusion rules, controlled retained evaluations and graduation decisions remain on the [roadmap](development-roadmap.md).

## Student portal

**My campus → Degree progress** (`/portal/student/progress/`) offers the signed-in student's own program pursuits, including closed pursuits and separate dual-degree choices. Program/entry-term labels distinguish choices. Choices use indexed 20-record pages without academic replay; selecting a pursuit requests one bounded review through the same canonical `DegreeProgressService` used by staff. The portal does not implement a second GPA, repeat or placement calculation.

The student view shows retained degree/year/version context, general minimum comparisons, approved minimum reductions, group quantities and a complete proposed placement when available. Unknown GPA or missing evidence remains unavailable, not zero. Read/search limits do not produce partial totals or a speculative group plan. Earlier-history and changed-cohort notices remain visible. These are current, non-retained reviews of available sources; closed-pursuit access does not turn them into historical graduation results.

The shared dockable guide opens group details and locally paged academic-work treatment from the captured result. Contribution rows show retained course/term labels, academic state and independent credit/cumulative-GPA/overall-GPA treatment. They omit internal identities, approval reasons, actors, raw rules, event payloads and alternative-source references. Staff reasons and raw requirement grammar never enter the student response. The view does not certify degree completion or permit academic changes.

The Grades screen links here for GPA rather than adding raw transcript rows in JavaScript. The removed calculation could ignore retained repeat and transfer policies. Grades and assignment-score views otherwise retain their existing workflows.

Private, CSRF-protected POST routes:

- `/portal/api/students/{student}/degree-programs/` — owner-scoped pursuit choices, with an optional ULID cursor.
- `/portal/api/students/{student}/programs/{assignment}/degree-progress/` — one owner-scoped review. Requests cannot override requirements, policy or replay budgets.

Ownership and assignment scope are verified before and after the read. Broad staff/super authority does not bypass portal ownership; staff review other students through the authorized workspace. Routes retain private/no-store responses and debug suppression. No provider call, browser storage, academic write, new grant or migration is introduced.
