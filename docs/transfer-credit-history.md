# Transfer credit and retained award decisions

New transfer awards, reasoned credit/grade amendments, voids and reinstatements now use the existing **`sis_history_events`** journal. `TransferCredit` owns one stream per award ULID, retaining the linked credit ULID throughout its lifetime. Current academic-credit and award rows are projections of recorded decisions. This extends [institutional credit history](academic-credit-history.md); it does not introduce another event store.

## Registrar workflow

Use **Transfers → Equivalencies → Review award** or the connected award action on a student's transcript. Initial award entry still reviews the equivalency, sending institution, completion date, received grade rank and posting term. A repeat submission for the same student, equivalency and completion date returns the same active credit; a different posting term conflicts. A voided award requires explicit reinstatement.

Corrections require both `sis:transfer:manage` and `sis:student:read`. Catalog management, ordinary staff membership and teaching authority do not imply these registrar duties. Review credits/grade or a void/reinstatement, supply the reason and effective date, then confirm. The effective date defaults to today and must be between the external course's completion date and today. It expresses when the correction applies; it does not claim that the institution knew the decision before its recorded timestamp.

**Change correction** preserves the entered date, reason, grade and credit units. An interrupted confirmation keeps its operation identity; retry or check the saved receipt. Exact accepted retries cannot overwrite later amendments or a void. Current authorization is rechecked after waiting for locks. A competing correction, different payload, different actor or changed reviewed identity requires a new review.

A void retains the award identity, source facts and previous decisions while removing its current attempted/earned units and GPA contribution. Reinstatement retains the last known published grading policy even if a newer scale was approved while the award was voided. Changing the awarded course, original completion identity or posting term remains a separate replacement workflow.

## Retained facts and history

The genuine award decision captures institutional course code/transcript title, credit units, student/course/term references, sending institution and external course identities/labels, completion date, actual received grade rank, the reviewed equivalency fields and the actual selected institutional grade definition. The source rule's grade reference and the student's resolved grade publication are retained separately: a program override can give the same grade code a different value.

Later edits to reference labels, equivalency settings or grading scales do not rewrite that decision. Replay calculates amended earned units and credit-weighted quality points from the selected retained grade facts. Current-source reference screens remain editable under their existing permissions; the posted-award screen identifies its retained source details. Full published course/classification bindings for transfers remain future work; a current code/title snapshot does not claim an uncaptured earlier publication.

**Correction history** remains a bounded registrar-only view with newest academic revisions first. Journal-backed entries obtain before/after facts, reason, effective time and actor from verified replay, rather than another stored copy of those academic facts. Ordinary student reads and Report Studio cannot access raw streams or protected reasons. Previously prepared/issued transcript documents remain unchanged; a changed academic projection invalidates a stale prepared document for delivery under the existing fulfillment checks. Existing enrollments and charges are preserved.

## Older awards and saved receipts

Reads and migrations do not invent award events for old current-state rows. Their first actual reviewed correction records an explicit observation followed by the real amendment/void/reinstatement in the same transaction. The observation retains the then-existing credit counters and grade facts, with its current actor and observation time. Earlier decision authors/times, received rank and earlier equivalency policy stay unknown. Labels observed now are not presented as proof of what a sending institution was called when the old award was posted.

Existing encrypted rows in `sis_transfer_award_changes` are preserved byte-for-byte and remain readable and retryable. The history screen labels them **Earlier saved receipt · effective time unknown**. They are evidence of the previously saved correction, not fabricated canonical events. An older untracked void without a retained grade reference cannot claim a known previous grading policy; its reviewed reinstatement selects an available passing policy and retains that real new decision.

New change rows in that existing table contain receipt/index metadata and an encrypted request fingerprint/review revision (`transfer-receipt.v2`). They do not store another before/after plan or reason. Academic facts and reasons live in the shared protected journal. New receipt reads/retries verify their award, credit, actor, action and academic revision against the corresponding journal decision. Old receipts use their preserved earlier format and explicitly limited provenance.

## Deployment and remaining work

Apply `20261109000004_CreateSisTransferCreditStreams` before deploying this write path. It creates only `sis_transfer_credit_streams`, a reconstructible award/student/credit routing marker with ULID identities. There are no backfill loops, additional event tables, destructive conversions or role-grant changes. A tracked record with missing/altered routing, award metadata or credit projection fails closed. Web requests do not repair it silently. The down migration refuses to discard retained routing.

The registered version-1 contracts are `transfer-credit-was-established` and `transfer-credit-was-changed`. They use the same authenticated journal, expected-version append, protected context and operation receipts as the rest of the application. Initial award, correction/observation, SQL projections, retry metadata and operational audit share their unit of work. Verified reads serialize with the student's award writers, and replay uses bounded pages. Replay performs no billing, prerequisite side effects, sends or new business decisions.

Fictional integration checks cover initial/correction replay, retained labels and grading policies, void/reinstatement, effective dates, current authority, stale/duplicate requests, unchanged prior event/receipt bytes, old receipt access, audit-failure rollback, tamper rejection and reconstruction in an isolated staging database. Provider/router checks exercise real dependency wiring, CSRF and private responses. Browser checks cover explicit review, date preservation, interrupted retries, escaped reasons, mobile layout and automated accessibility scans. All 97 SIS table definitions compile through the five installed schema dialects; runtime integration uses SQLite. Live cross-engine locking/LOB verification, operational rebuild/swap tools, retention operations, request/waitlist histories and lifecycle conversions remain outstanding. [Accepted registration and releases](registration-history.md) now have protected enrollment histories and section decision order. Development tooling has not migrated the working database.
