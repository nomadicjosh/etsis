# Transfer credit and retained award decisions

The shared [academic-details review](transfer-academic-periods.md) records or corrects the local posting term and the sending institution's instructional start and actual completion in this same transfer-credit stream. It preserves original award identity, prior date facts and receipts without changing awarded credits or grade. **Academic history** is curated and paged separately from credit/grade correction receipts. Retained source chronology supports the selected repeat policy; unknown periods are never filled from today's calendar.

New transfer awards, reasoned credit/grade amendments, voids and reinstatements now use the existing **`sis_history_events`** journal. `TransferCredit` owns one stream per award ULID, retaining the linked credit ULID throughout its lifetime. Current academic-credit and award rows are projections of recorded decisions. This extends [institutional credit history](academic-credit-history.md); it does not introduce another event store.

## Registrar workflow

Posted awards keep correction and course/academic-details review actions above the current award values. **More** groups **Correction history**, **Course definition history**, **Academic history** and **Void this award**. A void still requires its reason, proposed-outcome review and explicit confirmation. A voided award offers **Reinstate this award** directly. These controls use the retained award context and do not change catalog equivalencies.

Use **Transfers → Equivalencies → Review award** or the connected award action on a student's transcript. Initial award entry reviews the equivalency, sending institution, completion date, received grade rank and posting term, and requires an explicitly selected [accepted course publication](transfer-course-publications.md). A repeat submission for the same student, equivalency and completion date returns the same active credit; a different posting term or original opening publication conflicts. Later acceptance corrections do not change the original retry contract. A voided award requires explicit reinstatement.

New awards and corrections require both `sis:transfer:manage` and `sis:student:read`. Catalog management, ordinary staff membership and teaching authority do not imply these registrar duties. Review credits/grade or a void/reinstatement, supply the reason and effective date, then confirm. The effective date defaults to today and must be between the external course's completion date and today. It expresses when the correction applies; it does not claim that the institution knew the decision before its recorded timestamp.

**Change correction** preserves the entered date, reason, grade and credit units. An interrupted confirmation keeps its operation identity; retry or check the saved receipt. Exact accepted retries cannot overwrite later amendments or a void. New reviews expire after **20 minutes** and are bound to their staff member, award, action, operation and complete reviewed revision. Current authorization is rechecked after waiting for locks and again after the required audit. Expiry during confirmation rolls the entire correction back. A competing correction, different payload, different actor or changed reviewed identity requires a new review.

A void retains the award identity, source facts and previous decisions while removing its current attempted/earned units and GPA contribution. Reinstatement retains the last known published grading policy even if a newer scale was approved while the award was voided. The existing **Review accepted course** flow now supports a reasoned receiving-course replacement with a deliberately selected published definition. Opening course identity/publication, awarded units and original equivalency remain preserved. **Academic details** now corrects the posting term and source chronology together, while opening term and completion identities remain reserved for original award retries. A [reviewed course-acceptance correction](transfer-course-publications.md#review-an-existing-awards-accepted-course) can select a different publication or receiving course for this award, retaining prior identities, references and labels.

## Check a selected award's integrity

Choose **More → Check selected academic history** from an award. This compares its underlying academic credit and source projections with available protected history, including recorded void/correction facts. All three duties are required: `sis:history:verify`, `sis:audit:read`, `sis:student:read`. An award-management duty alone does not grant support access. The check uses the existing restricted receipts and fixed browser allowance; **Back to record** rereads the selected award. Earlier unknown history stays unknown, and no correction is made. The [selected academic check documentation](event-history-foundation.md#selected-institutional-and-transfer-academic-checks) explains incomplete outcomes and `sis:history:verify --credit=ULID`. That command takes the **academic credit reference**, not the award ID.

## Review security and replay limits

Correction, void and reinstatement share the existing review/confirmation editor and `ReviewProof` helper used by other registrar workflows. The preview returns `revision`, a server-issued ULID `operation_id` and an encrypted `review_token`. Confirmation sends these with the exact reviewed request. The revision still detects changed academic facts, identity labels, grading context or reasons; the proof binds that revision to its staff member, award, action and operation for 20 minutes. A pending old hash without a proof must be reviewed again.

The confirmation screen explains expiry. **Change correction** preserves credits, grade, date and reason; reviewing again obtains a fresh operation/proof. An interrupted save retries the original operation and proof or uses the existing **Check saved correction** action. It does not allocate another operation while checking a receipt. Delayed preview responses cannot reopen a closed drawer or supersede a newer review.

A completed operation keeps its original receipt contract. Exact retries return that result after expiry or later corrections/voids without changing newer facts. Existing accepted receipts remain retryable without the new proof when their actor, award, original request fingerprint and revision match. This is receipt verification, not permission to start another correction. Both existing receipt formats remain unchanged, and current registrar/student-read permissions still apply. No event, historical reason, saved request fingerprint or receipt is converted.

New mutations recheck the signed-in actor and both required permissions after lock acquisition and after the required audit. The proof is checked before the first observation/decision append and again after the audit. Expiry, audit failure, changed authority or a different staff account rolls back the event, any older observation, current academic projection, routing and receipt together. Private award/history reads and previews also recheck their actor/authority after required auditing.

Selected transfer replay now always has the existing **400-page / 2,000-event** ceiling. Correction preview, confirmation, history and receipt verification share one allowance across their repeated reads; a paged correction history remains limited to 50 selected receipts. An oversized verification stops with the existing private office-review notice, without accepting a partial result or changing academic data. Course-publication/source-period reviews keep their existing explicit bounds. Ordinary listings continue using projections; no directory-wide replay or repair process is added.

This extension adds no migration, table, permission or parallel event store. The configured application database was inspected read-only before implementation (20 events, no enrollments, migration `20261117000018`); live academic records were not updated. Fictional tests cover all three actions, missing/expired proofs, actor/operation/payload binding, completion-time rollback on tracked/older records, preserved receipts and financial facts, bounded continuation, actual CSRF-protected private routes and controller retry/edit behavior. Full browser layout and production-scale load tests remain open.

## Retained facts and history

The genuine award decision captures institutional course code/transcript title, credit units, student/course/term references, sending institution and external course identities/labels, completion date, actual received grade rank, the reviewed equivalency fields and the actual selected institutional grade definition. The source rule's grade reference and the student's resolved grade publication are retained separately: a program override can give the same grade code a different value.

Later edits to reference labels, equivalency settings or grading scales do not rewrite that decision. Replay calculates amended earned units and credit-weighted quality points from the selected retained grade facts. Current-source reference screens remain editable under their existing permissions; the posted-award screen identifies its retained source details. New awards retain the explicitly selected published course/classification definition and verified credit-version index. Earlier awards remain unbound where that evidence was not recorded; a label snapshot does not establish an uncaptured earlier publication. See [accepted course publications](transfer-course-publications.md).

**Correction history** remains a bounded registrar-only view with newest academic revisions first. Journal-backed entries obtain before/after facts, reason, effective time and actor from verified replay, rather than another stored copy of those academic facts. Ordinary student reads and Report Studio cannot access raw streams or protected reasons. Previously prepared/issued transcript documents remain unchanged; a changed academic projection invalidates a stale prepared document for delivery under the existing fulfillment checks. Existing enrollments and charges are preserved.

## Older awards and saved receipts

Reads and migrations do not invent award events for old current-state rows. Their first actual reviewed correction records an explicit observation followed by the real amendment/void/reinstatement in the same transaction. The observation retains the then-existing credit counters and grade facts, with its current actor and observation time. Earlier decision authors/times, received rank and earlier equivalency policy stay unknown. Labels observed now are not presented as proof of what a sending institution was called when the old award was posted.

Existing encrypted rows in `sis_transfer_award_changes` are preserved byte-for-byte and remain readable and retryable. The history screen labels them **Earlier saved receipt · effective time unknown**. They are evidence of the previously saved correction, not fabricated canonical events. An older untracked void without a retained grade reference cannot claim a known previous grading policy; its reviewed reinstatement selects an available passing policy and retains that real new decision.

New change rows in that existing table contain receipt/index metadata and an encrypted request fingerprint/review revision (`transfer-receipt.v2`). They do not store another before/after plan or reason. Academic facts and reasons live in the shared protected journal. New receipt reads/retries verify their award, credit, actor, action and academic revision against the corresponding journal decision. Old receipts use their preserved earlier format and explicitly limited provenance.

## Deployment and remaining work

Apply `20261109000004_CreateSisTransferCreditStreams` before deploying this write path. It creates only `sis_transfer_credit_streams`, a reconstructible award/student/credit routing marker with ULID identities. There are no backfill loops, additional event tables, destructive conversions or role-grant changes. A tracked record with missing/altered routing, award metadata or credit projection fails closed. Web requests do not repair it silently. The down migration refuses to discard retained routing.

The registered version-1 contracts are `transfer-credit-was-established` and `transfer-credit-was-changed`. They use the same authenticated journal, expected-version append, protected context and operation receipts as the rest of the application. Initial award, correction/observation, SQL projections, retry metadata and operational audit share their unit of work. Verified reads serialize with the student's award writers, and replay uses bounded pages. Replay performs no billing, prerequisite side effects, sends or new business decisions.

Fictional integration checks cover initial/correction replay, retained labels and grading policies, void/reinstatement, effective dates, current authority, stale/duplicate requests, unchanged prior event/receipt bytes, old receipt access, audit-failure rollback, tamper rejection and reconstruction in an isolated staging database. Provider/router checks exercise real dependency wiring, CSRF and private responses. Browser checks cover explicit review, date preservation, interrupted retries, escaped reasons, mobile layout and automated accessibility scans. All 97 SIS table definitions compile through the five installed schema dialects; runtime integration uses SQLite. Live cross-engine locking/LOB verification, operational rebuild/swap tools, retention operations, request/waitlist histories and lifecycle conversions remain outstanding. [Accepted registration and releases](registration-history.md) now have protected enrollment histories and section decision order. Development tooling has not migrated the working database.
