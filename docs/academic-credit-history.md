# Institutional credits and grade history

Institutional credits now have a replayable history from accepted registration through final-grade decisions and reviewed early/late drops. These events use the existing **`sis_history_events`** journal. There is no additional event store. [Transfer awards and corrections](transfer-credit-history.md) now also use the shared journal. [Accepted enrollment/seat decisions](registration-history.md) now have their own replayable history and committed section order. Full section-definition versions and student/program transitions remain staged work.

## Staff workflow

Faculty use **My teaching → Section → Grade & attendance → Record final grade**. Select a retained institutional grade, effective date and optional note, then review and explicitly confirm. A dual-degree student with different captured policies requires an explicit program-pursuit choice. Assignments, attendance and assignment scores remain separate from authoritative final-grade decisions.

Authorized records officers use **Students → Student record → View transcript → Review final grade**. A posted grade requires both `sis:gradebook:amend` and `sis:student:read`, with a mandatory correction reason. The correction permission is a separate protected system definition; broad teaching, registration and student-read permissions do not imply it. Assign it deliberately to the appropriate registrar staff. Initial grades require the assigned faculty or `sis:gradebook:manage`; correction permission alone cannot submit an initial grade. Super administrators retain their universal authority.

The review shows the before/after grade, grade-point units, effective date and reason. **Change review** preserves entered fields. Confirmation rechecks actor, authority, active enrollment, protected history, current projection, retained policy and expected stream version inside the transaction. A competing decision requires a new review. Exact retries return the original receipt, including after a later correction, without overwriting the newer grade. Current correction support covers active institutional enrollments; changing a closed enrollment, awarded course, credits or posting term needs a separate reasoned replacement workflow.

**Grade history** provides bounded pages of curated decisions with effective/recorded timestamps. Student owners, assigned faculty and staff with academic-read authority can read the authorized record. Reasons and actor identifiers are withheld from this ordinary history view; raw protected streams have no browser/reporting endpoint. An early-dropped current record is not resurrected through this view.

## Historical facts and policies

A credit stream is keyed by its academic-credit ULID. Its opening event records the accepted enrollment, student, section and term references, credit units, course code/transcript title, available published course reference and explicit applicable grading-publication references. A later scale publication or program-pursuit closure does not change a known captured policy, even before the credit receives its first grade. Unknown inception policy remains explicitly unknown; the first reviewed grade chooses an actual available policy. Already graded records retain their exact grade publication.

Grade decisions retain the selected definition's code, per-course points, GPA/credit flags and scale/publication references. Replay calculates earned credit and credit-weighted quality points from these retained facts. It does not ask today's catalog or policy to reinterpret an old decision. Effective business dates may be earlier than the recording date, from the enrollment date through today. Effective dates do not imply that the institution knew the decision before it was recorded.

Existing untracked credits are **not** backfilled by a migration or read. Their first reviewed grade/drop records an explicit observation of existing facts, attributed to the current action, followed by the actual decision in the same transaction. Earlier author/action dates and policy provenance remain unknown. Existing earned units, grade references, quality points and row revision are preserved in that observation. A missing or altered tracked projection fails closed; it is never silently reclassified as a legacy baseline.

Early drop removes current enrollment/credit/course-version projections and reverses the original charge using the existing billing workflow, while retaining restricted academic events. Late drop retains attempted credit, its resolved drop grade/GPA rule and the charge. A retake/new registration receives a different credit identity. Hiatus decisions use the same drop service and ledger. Existing encrypted drop/hiatus receipts remain evidence and are not rewritten into invented earlier events.

## Storage and deployment

Apply `20261109000003_CreateSisAcademicCreditStreams` through the normal migration process before deploying this write path. It creates **`sis_academic_credit_streams`**, a reconstructible credit/enrollment/student/section routing projection, plus the protected correction permission. It does not convert current academic rows, copy event bodies or grant a role additional authority. Routing survives early deletion and deliberately has no foreign key to the deleted current credit/enrollment. The migration is retryable and refuses to overwrite an existing custom permission of the same name. Down migration refuses to discard retained stream routing.

The registered version-1 event contracts are `institutional-credit-was-established`, `final-grade-was-decided` and `institutional-credit-was-dropped`. Each append uses expected version, operation identity and protected command context. Observation plus grade/drop, projection changes, audits and any related registration billing share the unit of work. Old event contracts and recorded bytes/receipts remain unchanged. Reads replay bounded pages and compare typed projected facts with protected history; curated paging freezes its committed head.

Fictional SQLite checks cover root/Codefy replay, byte/receipt preservation, policy pinning, permission/reason/date checks, cross-record/actor tokens, concurrent reviews, exact retries, atomic failures, early-drop retention and rebuilding credit projections without billing/event side effects. Browser checks use fictional data, CSRF, explicit confirmation, field preservation, narrow-screen reflow and automated accessibility scans. Schema compilation covers the five installed framework dialects; live cross-engine locking, isolated rebuild/swap tooling, external integrity anchors and retention operations remain acceptance work. Development tooling has not migrated the working database or run a live projection rebuild.
