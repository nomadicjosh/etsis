# Public campus events

From **Spaces & scheduling**, open a standalone room or online booking and choose **Manage public event**. Publishing requires both `sis:scheduling:manage` and `sis:portal:manage`; owning a booking alone does not permit public publication. The institution can grant these duties together to its event publishers. Reservations still use the existing request, approval, cancellation and rescheduling workflow.

1. Approve the booking and resolve its requested dates.
2. Enter a separate **Public event title** and **Public event description**. The internal purpose is never copied into either field. Public content is plain text; the portal escapes it.
3. Choose **Review public event**. Review the public words, retained room/building labels and every active meeting time, twenty dates at a time. The review uses the institution's timezone and expires after twenty minutes.
4. Choose **Publish public event**. Confirmation locks and checks the approved source, location, current editorial revision, actor and both permissions again. The public calendar link opens the resulting portal view.
5. Edit and review again to update public content. **Withdraw public event** removes it from public results while keeping the reservation and its history. Withdrawal remains available if the booking was subsequently cancelled.

Public publishing accepts approved standalone room or online bookings with no course-section link and between one and 366 active occurrences. Class-linked physical and online meetings remain in the private academic timetable. Neither approving a booking nor publishing an academic meeting plan makes it a public event.

## Online events

In **Plan a meeting**, select **Online meeting without a room** and leave **Course section** empty for a standalone event. The same choice carries into the recurring planner. Scheduling authority is required through completion; a regular account cannot create an unlinked online booking. Approval confirms the schedule without reserving a room or checking student/faculty availability. Linked classes still require a published online/hybrid offering and valid instructional dates.

Use **Manage public event** after approval, entering and reviewing the separate public invitation as above. Public locations display **Online event** and **Joining details provided by the organizer**. No private conferencing link, credential or joining instruction is copied from the booking purpose. The public calendar, search and native Vihzhuo feed share these safe labels. A move between online and physical locations uses the existing reviewed reschedule flow, preserving the original meeting; publish its replacement explicitly if it should be public.

API booking and reschedule inputs require an explicit `room_id`: a room identity, or `null`/an empty selection for online meetings. Omitting the field is rejected instead of silently creating an online event. Standalone recurring creation serializes on the authenticated requester's account identity, while existing room and class requests retain their room/section locks. Original retries preserve later approvals and cancellations.

## Public calendar and source checks

The public calendar at `/portal/events/` offers full civil months or Monday–Sunday weeks, event/location search, day choices and twenty-event daily pages. It initially opens the requested calendar date; day and page changes preserve filters. Server-rendered links/forms work without JavaScript. AJAX replaces the same escaped calendar fragment, restores keyboard focus and ignores responses superseded by a newer selection or search draft. No profile, finance, roster or account overview is requested. Dates use actual timezone offsets and the shared civil-day model, including overnight meetings and daylight-saving changes.

Only explicitly published content and times appear. Public reads rejoin the approved source booking and uncancelled occurrence, matching its retained room or online location and exact times on both the booking and occurrence. A stored location mismatch hides that time and must be corrected before a new publication review. Cancelling a single occurrence hides that occurrence and keeps other published dates visible. Cancelling the whole booking hides all its times. A rescheduled replacement is a separate booking and is not published automatically; review its public invitation explicitly. Room/building labels retain what the publisher reviewed until a later review updates them. Internal categories, people, private purposes, review tokens and source booking/occurrence identities are omitted. Public identities are distinct from private source identities.

The **Live public campus events** Vihzhuo block shares those safe reads and shows up to three event times in the next 31 days, linking to the corresponding calendar day. Its heading is editable and its cache is disabled. New generated campus pages include the block; existing CMS pages remain unchanged, and their editors can add it from **Campus portal** blocks. All three campus themes inherit the calendar navigation and progressive enhancement.

## Public calendar subscriptions and downloads

The optional calendar feed adds **Follow the public calendar** below `/portal/events/`. It is available in all three bundled campus themes, including without JavaScript. Visitors can download `campus-events.ics` or copy the labelled feed address into their calendar application's **Subscribe by URL** option. It requires no account or secret link.

To enable it, set `PORTAL_PUBLIC_EVENT_FEED_ENABLED=true` in the deployment environment. Its default is `false`; `config/portal.php` exposes the same `public_event_feed.enabled` setting. Use a Boolean or the exact values `true`, `false`, `1` or `0`. Keep `APP_BASE_URL` set to the installation's canonical HTTP(S) base URL, using HTTPS for a public deployment. The application constructs the feed address from that setting, never the request's Host or forwarding headers. No database migration or additional worker is required. The configured development environment is left unchanged.

The public endpoint is `/portal/events/calendar.ics`. Its complete rolling window starts at institutional midnight seven days before today and ends exclusively at midnight 90 days after today. It includes overlapping meetings with their actual full start/end times. The feed covers **all** matching public events, independently of the calendar page's current date, day, search or daily page. It does not represent the visitor's classes, advising appointments or private bookings. Room-free events contain only their reviewed public location wording, without conferencing credentials.

Feed reads use the calendar's same live approved-booking, standalone-source, active-occurrence, retained-location and exact-time joins. Cancellation, withdrawal, later class linkage or mismatched source times/locations removes the affected meeting from subsequent responses. Republishing the same meeting keeps its opaque calendar UID and increases its publication sequence; rebuilding its disposable public-time row does not create a duplicate calendar identity. A separately reviewed rescheduled replacement has a distinct identity and requires explicit public publication as before. Source booking/occurrence identities, staff purposes, account details and journal payloads are withheld.

Files use [RFC 5545 iCalendar](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc5545.html), UTC instants, the real publication timestamp, UTF-8 line folding and escaped plain text. Public-calendar links select the meeting's institutional local day, including when it differs from its UTC date. Meetings are transparent entries with no invitations, attendees, organizer addresses or automatic alarms. Text cannot introduce additional calendar components or executable HTML fields.

A **downloaded snapshot does not update**. A calendar application's subscription refreshes on that application's schedule; removal and older-copy retention depend on the client. Withdrawal cannot recall an already downloaded or cached public invitation. Check the live campus calendar before attending, and refresh or remove an obsolete external copy when necessary. This feed sends no email/push cancellation message and makes no external provider request. Institution-selected client compatibility and refresh behavior remain deployment acceptance work.

Reads probe at most 501 matching times and return at most 500, with a two-MiB output ceiling. Over-limit output and failures during feed generation return a complete HTTP503 retry response with `Retry-After: 300`; no truncated calendar is issued. Operators should review public-event volume/source consistency rather than repeatedly importing a partial file. The ordinary bounded week/month calendar remains available. Disabled feeds return HTTP404 and have no visible controls; malformed settings hide the controls and return HTTP503. GET and HEAD responses retain private/no-store and debug-suppression protections. Feed reads create no notifications, recipient records, source edits or historical events.

## Event announcements

After publishing, choose **Prepare announcement** in the same public-event screen. This also requires `sis:communications:manage`. The existing Communications editor opens with the published title, description, retained location labels and all currently public meeting times in the publication's timezone, including explicit UTC offsets. Unpublished edits, private booking purposes and conferencing details are not copied. Cancelled, withdrawn or mismatched meetings are excluded using the same approved-source checks as the public calendar; an event with no active public dates cannot prepare an announcement. The read checks all three permissions again before returning and never creates recipients, drafts or deliveries by itself.

Review or edit the message, then choose **Save and continue** to create an encrypted Communications draft under your account. Follow the shared [recipient-review and deliberate-delivery workflow](communications.md#drafts-and-recipient-review) to select people, confirm the exact draft revision and queue delivery references. Preparing or saving the draft never sends a message. Reusable templates and their private library are described in the same Communications guide.

An announcement is an editable copy. Subsequent event edits, cancellations or withdrawal do not rewrite a saved or queued message; review dates again before queueing or sending. This is a deliberate staff announcement, not an automatic subscription or cancellation-alert service. The public calendar remains the current source for event availability. No additional message store, delivery queue or event journal is introduced.

Current content and its published-time projection commit together with required audit and revision checks; no booking, academic or financial event is fabricated or rewritten. A repeated accepted publication returns its saved result after withdrawal without publishing again. An older review after a later publication is rejected rather than restoring earlier content.
