## Presentation and navigation finish criteria

Inset form, summary and task panels in drawers use white backgrounds against the larger gray drawer canvas. Long labels and values must wrap; tables that genuinely compare columns may remain accessible horizontal regions. Return actions must identify their destination and retain the appropriate accepted page or unsaved draft. Pending/unavailable controls, ordinary failures, changed authority and late responses must leave a coherent usable view. Check keyboard use, phone widths and enlarged text, including menus near viewport edges and the sidebar. Record the actual scope tested before moving back to roadmap implementation.
