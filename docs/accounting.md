## Financials and Financial Aid

### Set up your Chart of Accounts

The Chart of Accounts lists the institution's financial accounts that are connected with their accounts receivable.

etSIS should come with Accounts Receivable (A/R) with the option of changing the A/R account's number and description.

The built-in A/R account should be hardwired into all the relevant transactions in etSIS Financial, and cannot be disconnected from etSIS financial functions.

Other types of accounts would include liability accounts for financial aid awards, income accounts for special fees or donations, asset accounts for different payment types, and so on.

### Financial Aid Award Types

Award types are the various financial aid awards you give to students, whether grants or loans, whether from a government entity, your school, or some other scholarship fund. As you prepare your school's financial aid setup, you should review the built-in Federal awards and add any additional aid types your school awards.

#### Liability and asset Accounts

Aid awards require you to link the award to a liability account and gives you the option of also linking to an asset account.

How it works:

- When you select a liability account, you are selecting the account that is *debited* when the award is disbursed to the student. The built-in Accounts Receivable account on your Chart of Accounts is automatically credited in that transaction, which decreases the student's balance. 
- When you select an asset account, you are selecting the account that is *credited* when you issue an aid refund to the student (not to the aid source). The built-in Accounts Receivable account on your Chart of Accounts is automatically debited in that transaction, which increases the student's balance. 
- The built-in A/R account should handle the relevant portions of these transactions.

#### etSIS Native Award Types

* FWS: Federal Work Study (COD)
* PLUS: Federal PLUS Loan (COD)
* PELL: Pell Grants (COD)
* DLSUB & DLUNSUB: Direct Loans, subsidized and unsubsidized (COD)
* FSEOG: Supplemental Educational Opportunity Grant
* TEACH: Teacher Education Assistance for College and Higher Education Grant
* VAB: VA Benefits

Before you can offer a built-in Federal award, you need to first associate it with an asset account and a liability account; a Financial user can set these up in Chart of Accounts.
