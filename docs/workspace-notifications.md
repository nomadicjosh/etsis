# Workspace and portal toast notifications

Implemented November 23, 2026. Staff and signed-in portal action feedback use the same locally hosted Bootstrap Toast component, colors, icons and accessible controls. The four supplied sound files are selectable. The same bell provides [campus announcements](campus-announcements.md), [private waitlist outcome updates](registration-history.md#private-waitlist-outcome-updates), [private advising updates](academic-advising.md#private-advising-updates), import results, [final-grade updates](#private-final-grade-updates) and [transcript updates](#private-transcript-updates) and [standing updates](#private-standing-updates), with deletion controls. Further automatic domain triggers and push/email delivery remain separate roadmap work.

## Use notifications

Successful workspace actions can show **Completed**, **Update** or **Needs attention** feedback. A toast never moves keyboard focus when it appears. Use **Dismiss notification** to close it. Ordinary toasts disappear after eight seconds; Bootstrap pauses dismissal while the pointer or keyboard focus is within the toast.

The bell in the workspace header opens **Recent workspace notifications**. It retains the newest twenty messages from this page, even after their toasts disappear. The badge combines recent page feedback with unread academic, standing and transcript notices and, in the workspace, unread import notices. It is not an institution-wide unread count. **Clear recent notifications** removes this temporary feedback only. It does not cancel jobs, delete audit/history or change recorded decisions. Closing the panel returns focus to the bell.

Expand **Campus announcements** in this panel for reviewed campus news and account-specific read state. It loads separately on demand and retains its read state on the server. Clearing recent action feedback does not clear saved notices. Notification sections show all retained notices with paging and deletion; reopening a section reloads its current list. There are no sound settings, show-read filters, mark-read buttons or refresh toolbars in the bell. See [publication, reading and sound behavior](campus-announcements.md).

Only five toasts are visible at once. Further feedback remains available in the recent list. Toasts render as literal text, including any angle brackets in a message. They appear within the active drawer, modal or native report dialog so they remain visible and their dismiss controls work with the active focus boundary. In Bootstrap drawers, feedback occupies its own bounded footer space so it does not cover the next action button. The drawer body keeps its own scrolling; an empty notification region occupies no extra space. The recent panel, cards and controls reflow on narrow screens and with enlarged text. Toasts do not animate.

## Delete notifications

Personal notifications are disposable operational information and require no historical archive:

- **Delete notification** removes one recent action message and its remaining toast from page memory. **Clear recent notifications** removes all recent action feedback. These messages were never saved to the database.
- **Registration updates → Delete update**, **Advising updates → Delete update**, **Academic updates → Delete update**, **Transcript updates → Delete update**, **Academic standing updates → Delete update** and **Import updates → Delete notification** physically delete the saved personal notice and its read metadata. Reloading or retrying the original accepted command does not recreate it. Read notices remain listed until deleted. Opening an announcement, advising check-in, academic record, standing detail or transcript request acknowledges its displayed notice revision automatically; no separate read button is needed.
- **Campus announcements → Delete announcement**, in either the list or opened item, removes the current publication from this account's drawer. Its read metadata is physically deleted. Only one current account/content/revision suppression marker remains so the shared publication cannot immediately reappear. The marker has no message body, reading timestamp or historical revision list. Other accounts and the underlying campus news are unaffected; a later reviewed republication can appear again.

There is no separate notification archive or trash screen. Deletion survives drawer reopening and reload where server state is involved. It never deletes actual academic, registration, financial, publication or other business decisions. Required minimal administrative audit remains independent of notification content. Delete/read confirmations use an inline live status rather than creating replacement recent feedback.

## Sound and display preferences

Open **People → person → More → Notification preferences** in the workspace. The drawer retains **Back to record**. In the student portal, open **Personal information → Notification preferences → Manage notification preferences**. Both locations edit the same person's settings, so saved choices follow their sign-in between browsers. Authorized person managers may edit another person's preferences without changing their own playback.

**Play notification sounds** defaults off. Choose **Short tone**, **Message**, **Warm success**, **Bird** or **Piano** and use **Test notification sound** to preview. Previewing or changing a choice neither enables sound nor saves it. Use **Save notification preferences** to apply the form, or **Close without saving** to discard unsaved choices. A stale save retains the draft and asks you to reopen the editor; it never overwrites a newer save. Short tone is synthesized; the other choices use the supplied files in `resources/sounds/`. Browser interaction may be required before audio can play; missing, unsupported or blocked audio leaves visual feedback working.

Ordinary sounds are limited to one every two seconds, including when several notifications await the same browser audio unlock or file load. A longer file plays alone; further notifications do not overlap it. Preview replaces any current sound. Background tabs remain silent. Applying muted/different preferences, changing a preview choice, closing its drawer, clearing private feedback, hiding the tab or leaving the page stops playback and cancels pending file loading. A delayed unlock/load cannot play an obsolete choice. Reopening recent history and dismissing a toast do not play sounds.

Files load on demand from a fixed same-origin allowlist, without credentials or redirects. Nothing is downloaded just by selecting a choice, loading a page or leaving sound off. One decoded file is retained per current audio context; concurrent requests share one pending load, with an eight-second deadline, a two-MiB file limit and an eight-second playback ceiling. The supplied files are all shorter/smaller than those limits. No external sound provider is contacted. `npm run build:assets` copies only the four named source files to `public/assets/sounds/`; deploy those generated assets with the application. The source files are preserved unchanged.

Enable **Keep notifications on screen until dismissed** to remove the automatic timeout. This preference applies to currently visible feedback as well as future messages. Keyboard/pointer reading and explicit dismissal remain available.

Only `{sound, keep_visible, sound_id}` is saved with the canonical person on the server. Owner checks, person-management authority, CSRF, expected revisions and required atomic metadata audit protect changes. These are current personal display settings, separate from academic history and communication consent. No preference history archive is created. The previous shared browser key is removed rather than assigned to an arbitrary signed-in person. Existing users therefore start quietly until they explicitly save person preferences. Notification content, identities, drafts and preferences are never stored in local or session storage. Sound IDs must come from the fixed allowlist; submitted URLs cannot select an audio source.

## Student and applicant portal

Signed-in campus pages have a **Recent notifications** bell, using the same notice, record-link and deletion controls. Sound and display settings are organized under **Personal information**, outside the bell. Guests have no private feedback panel or sound downloads. Contact updates, submitted applications, accepted registrations, section changes, drops/withdrawals and transcript request/cancellation acknowledgements use shared toasts. Their original CSRF, owner checks, review commands, preserved facts and request payloads remain in force. Queued transcript requests remain requests, not completed transcript delivery.

Action feedback is local to the current page, including when moving between portal services or opening the staff workspace. The separate collapsed announcement, registration, advising, academic, standing and transcript sections load their account-owned server state on demand. Unread academic, standing and transcript counts are checked once a minute while a signed-in page is visible; guests perform no private polling. Admissions updates, booking updates, records and request status retain their existing private service views. No message send or delivery worker is added by these notices. HTTP401/403 and page leave clear recent feedback and stop audio. Toast dismissal inside a modal restores a usable focus target; enlarged portal headings wrap on narrow screens. The assignment-score drawer now passes the portal's actual node factory to the shared gradebook presenter. Service panels retain a connected element so existing stale-response checks do not mistake a rendered page for a disconnected document fragment; replacing the service still disconnects the old panel.

## Connected background outcomes

Existing recipient-queue and delivery-authorization confirmations use informational feedback. **Recipients queued. No messages have been sent.** keeps queue acceptance distinct from delivery. These notifications do not send or retry messages.

An open communications progress screen can announce a transition from active processing to completed, held or blocked. A completed process may include outcomes closed without resending; the notification does not claim every message was sent. Held or blocked processing asks staff to review the existing source controls. Opening an already finished campaign or refreshing an unchanged outcome produces no duplicate completion notification.

Report Studio distinguishes **Export queued** from **Your report export is ready**. It announces genuine pending-to-ready, failed or cancelled changes and handles a preparation acknowledgement that is already ready. Opening a previously completed export does not replay an old completion alert. Existing generation/connected-view checks prevent an obsolete or closed report response from showing a new toast. The report's status and private download controls remain authoritative; no additional report, dataset, background poll or export is introduced.

## Privacy and validation

Temporary action messages live only in a bounded in-memory list. Reloading, leaving the page or signing out clears them. Saved updates and shared announcements have the separate server deletion rules above. Workspace HTTP 401/403 responses clear visible and recent feedback before existing sign-in/access-error handling continues. Toasts contain safe action/outcome wording, not message bodies, addresses, export rows, provider responses or raw events. Existing business authorization, CSRF, command reviews and audit/history remain unchanged.

Fictional browser checks cover bounded bursts, literal rendering, focus, timed/persistent feedback, sound opt-in/rate limits, local choices/caching, load timeout, delayed unlock/load and mute/choice/background races, blocked audio/storage, reload/privacy clearing, active drawer/modal/native-dialog placement, keyboard use and WCAG narrow/enlarged-text review. Audio playback uses injected contexts; actual WAV/MP3 decoding is checked in an offline browser audio context without device playback. Portal actions retain their original requests; export and communications checks use injected providers and confirm that feedback does not issue sending requests. Native device audio and broader institution-led browser/screen-reader qualification remain deployment acceptance work.


## Advising follow-up handoff

Expand **Advising updates** to review notices for your own student account, including when using the staff workspace. New check-ins and genuine staff/advisor resolution actions produce the latest disposable notice; your response marks an existing notice read. **View check-in** opens the exact owner-verified check-in and supports the existing response workflow independently of degree-progress availability. **Back to advising updates** preserves the current notification page. The list withholds conversation/private-note text, and opening details deliberately loads only the selected check-in. Read/delete confirmation stays inline and deletion creates no replacement toast. See [the workflow, privacy and retry rules](academic-advising.md#private-advising-updates).


## Private final-grade updates

Expand **Academic updates** in the workspace or signed-in portal bell. A newly posted final grade, direct correction or approved independent correction creates one personal **Final grade available** or **Final grade updated** notice. Pending, rejected and cancelled correction requests produce no grade notice. A later genuine correction replaces the earlier notice for that academic credit. Opening or retrying an accepted grading command, restoring a projection and reading old records never manufacture notifications.

The list shows course, section and term labels, without copying grades or correction reasons into notification storage. **View academic record** opens only this account's current verified academic record and displays its current final grade, term and completed credit quantity. Later corrections may have changed the grade since the notice was created. **Back to academic updates** returns to the list. **Delete update** permanently removes the disposable notice and read metadata; it preserves original grades, corrections, approvals and protected history. Retrying the original accepted grade action does not recreate a deleted notice. Account relinking never hands earlier notices to a replacement account.

Publication and the accepted grade share one transaction; failed required audit, source, recipient or final notice checks roll back both. Two additive portable tables store current person preferences and minimal academic-notice references. No earlier grade notice is backfilled, new academic event type is invented, parallel journal is created or delivery worker is launched. Further grade triggers and external delivery remain separate roadmap work.


## Private transcript updates

Expand **Transcript updates** in the workspace or signed-in portal bell. A newly accepted registrar preparation creates **Transcript prepared**; a separately accepted delivery record creates **Transcript delivery recorded**. Only the latest notice for that request is kept. New requests, cancelled pending requests, previews, review screens and document reads do not create notices. Preparation does not mean delivery or establish that an electronic message was sent.

**View transcript request** shows the requested date, delivery method and current request status. It contains no transcript content, grades, recipient address, release confirmation or delivery evidence and grants no document download. **Back to transcript updates** returns to the same notice page; **Open transcript requests** leads to the existing owner portal workflow. Opening the selected notice acknowledges only its displayed revision. Read notices remain listed until deleted. Sound/display preferences remain in the person drawer or portal Personal information, outside the bell.

**Delete update** physically removes the personal notice and read timestamp. Deletion survives reopening, reload and retries of the original accepted preparation/delivery, even after the review expires. It leaves requests, retained transcript originals, delivery evidence, required minimal audit and protected academic history intact. A genuinely later preparation or delivery may create a new notice. Replacing the person's linked account never transfers an earlier request's notices to the replacement account. Transcript-management permission alone cannot read another person's notifications.

The portable ULID notice table contains routing/status/version/timestamp references only. Publication shares the ordinary fulfillment transaction, locks and required audit; final request, latest-document metadata, canonical owner and notice checks prevent partial acceptance. Private paging uses twenty items and a timestamp/ID cursor, with separate indexes for unread and all-retained lists. Visible signed-in pages include unread transcript notices in the existing one-minute count check; guests do no private polling. No historical notices are backfilled, no authoritative event type or parallel journal is added, and no email, electronic delivery integration, new report or background worker is introduced.


## Private standing updates

Expand **Academic standing updates** in the workspace or signed-in portal bell. A genuine confirmed standing decision or GPA/load proposal produces **Academic standing updated**; an explicitly confirmed closed-pursuit correction produces **Historical academic standing corrected**. Only the latest notice per pursuit remains. Reviews, unconfirmed proposals, definition/policy edits, accepted retries and replay are silent, and earlier decisions are not backfilled.

**View academic standing** loads this account's verified retained codes, labels and inclusive dates. It includes past and future periods, explains a historical pursuit, and distinguishes no recorded periods from good standing. Program labels are current; staff reasons, policy references, GPA/load evidence and raw events are withheld. **Back to standing updates** preserves the current page. Opening a detail acknowledges its exact revision. Read notices remain until **Delete update** physically deletes the notice and read metadata while preserving all academic facts. Original accepted retries cannot recreate deleted notices.

The notice is pinned to the canonical person's account at confirmation. Staff/advisor permissions cannot read another person's notices, and a replacement account does not inherit them. Publication shares the accepted standing transaction and final source/owner checks. The portable ULID table stores references only; owner-scoped indexed pages contain at most twenty items with a timestamp/ID cursor. Unread counts join the existing visible-page, one-minute check. Preferences remain in the person editor or portal Personal information. Nothing private is stored in browser storage, and no email/push delivery, report, worker or separate history archive is added. See [the academic workflow and boundaries](academic-standing.md#private-standing-updates).
